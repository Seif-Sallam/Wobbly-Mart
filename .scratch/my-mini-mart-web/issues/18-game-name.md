# Name the game

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: Seif (claimed 2026-10-03)
Blocked by: —

## Question

What is the game called (must not use the original's name or branding), and what does its logo look like within the chosen palette and toy style?

## Context

Palette and look: [Pick the asset packs and art direction](08-art-direction-and-assets.md). Tone: wonky and funny.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-03 (grilling).

**Name: Wobbly Mart** — the Stack's wobble is the game's signature. "My Wobbly Mart" was dropped to avoid copying the original's "My ___ Mart" pattern. No game by that name found (nearest: *Wobbly Life*, *My Mart* — different names).

- Written "Wobbly Mart" (with a space) on the logo, tab title, home screen and Credits; `wobbly-mart` for the repo and Pages URL. The repo is renamed from `my-mini-mart`.
- No tagline.

**Logo:** flat SVG wordmark in Fredoka (no 3D), chunky and slightly tilted, letters at uneven heights like a swaying stack.

- "Wobbly" in Money green, "Mart" in warm orange, one shared dark-wood outline and a soft drop shadow. Exact values come from [Fix the named palette](20-palette.md).
- Stack icon: crate, tomato and egg as oversized flat shapes, tilted, sitting on the "M" of Mart. It must read at 32 px.
- The icon alone on a sky-blue rounded square is the favicon and the iPhone home-screen icon.

**Motion (title screen, in-house tweens):** drops in with a squash and a bouncy plop → letters bob gently out of sync, the stack sways like the in-game Stack → **Play** triggers one last big wobble, then the circle wipe.
